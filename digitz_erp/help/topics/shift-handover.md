# Handing over a counter

Use a handover when one cashier finishes and another continues on the **same counter and the same PC**. Each cashier has their own day, so each is accountable only for their own shift's cash.

## Outgoing cashier

1. Open **Day Close**, count the till and fill in **Counted Cash**.
2. Click **Close & Hand Over** and confirm.
3. Click **Print Day Close Slip** if you want a copy.
4. Click **Log Out for Next Cashier**.

The counter is now free, even if your count had a difference that still waits for a supervisor.

## Incoming cashier

1. Log in on the same PC.
2. Open **Day Open** (the badge takes you there).
3. Under **Previous Close on this Counter**, click **Take over this count**. The outgoing cashier's count fills your grid.
4. **Recount the notes in the till.** Change the grid if anything differs, and say why in **Remarks**.
5. Click **Open Day**.

## Things to know

- **You cannot skip the close.** While the outgoing cashier's day is open, Day Open shows *Counter is in use* and will not open.
- **If the outgoing cashier has already left without closing,** a supervisor closes the day for them. See [Supervisor tasks](#supervisor-tasks).
- **Different PC, same counter?** Only one day can be open on a counter at a time, whichever PC it is on. Hand over first.
- **Moving to another counter?** Close your day on the first counter, then open a new one on the second.
