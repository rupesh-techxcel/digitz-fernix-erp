# Closing your day

Close your day at the end of your shift, by counting the cash in the till. The system compares your count with the cash the till should hold.

## Steps

1. Submit anything still in draft that was paid in cash. Drafts do not count towards the till.
2. Click the badge at the top of the page, or open the **Cashier Console** from the Medical Center workspace and go to **Day Close**.
3. Check **Cash for the Day** on the left:

   | Line | What it is |
   |---|---|
   | Opening Float | What you opened with |
   | + Cash Sales | Cash Sales Invoices (not credit sales) |
   | + Cash Receipts (Credit Collections) | Cash Receipt Entries |
   | − Cash Refunds | Cash Sales Returns |
   | − Cash Paid Out | Cash Expense Entries |
   | − Expenditure | The cash you paid out of the till today, as you enter it in step 4 |
   | **Expected Cash in the Till** | What should be in the till now |

   Click any line to see the documents behind it, and click a document to open it.

   ![The Day Close page: Cash for the Day, the note count, and the Close buttons](/assets/digitz_erp/images/help/day-close.png)
4. Enter **Expenditure**: the total cash you paid out of the till today, for example for water, stationery or a taxi. Leave it empty if you paid nothing out. **Expected Cash in the Till** goes down by this amount as you type. Say what it was for in **Remarks**, and keep the bills with the cash.
5. Count the till in **Count the Closing Cash**. **Counted Cash** fills in from the count, or you can type the total.
6. Read the result under Counted Cash:
   - **The till matches the expected cash.** The day will close.
   - **Short by … / Over by …**: the day will wait for a supervisor. Write the reason in **Remarks**.
7. Click one of:
   - **Close Day**: you are done for the day.
   - **Close & Hand Over**: another cashier takes this counter now. This closes your day and logs you out. See [Handing over a counter](#shift-handover).
8. Click **Print Day Close Slip** and keep the slip with the cash. It shows the expenditure.

## When the count does not match

A difference is not an error. The day goes to **Closing** (*Close sent for approval*), and a supervisor reviews your remarks and approves it. Then the day is **Closed**.

While you wait:

- **The counter is free.** The next cashier can open on it straight away.
- **You** cannot open a new day until your close is approved.

## About expenditure

- It is **one amount for the whole day**, entered when you close. It is not entered during the day.
- It only lowers the cash the till should hold. **It is not posted to the accounts.** Give the bills to your supervisor or accountant so they can record the expenses.
- Once the day is closed, you cannot change it. A supervisor or System Manager corrects it by cancelling and amending the day.

## After closing

- The badge shows *Day not opened* (or *Close awaiting approval*).
- You cannot bill again until you open a new day.
- A closed day cannot be edited. If the count was wrong, a System Manager cancels and amends it. See [Problems and messages](#counter-troubleshooting).
