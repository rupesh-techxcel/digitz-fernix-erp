# Closing your day

Close your day at the end of your shift, by counting the cash in the till and entering your card, bank and other takings. This is a blind count: the page does not show the day's sales or the cash the till should hold. A supervisor checks your count against the system afterwards.

## Steps

1. Submit anything still in draft. Drafts do not count towards the day.
2. Click the badge at the top of the page, or open the **Cashier Console** from the Medical Center workspace and go to **Day Close**.
3. Count the till in **Count the Closing Cash**. **Counted Cash** fills in from the count, or you can type the total.
4. In **Card, Bank & Other Payments**, enter today's total for each payment mode, for example from the card machine's settlement slip. Leave a mode empty if you took nothing by it.
5. Enter **Expenditure**: the total cash you paid out of the till today, for example for water, stationery or a taxi. Leave it empty if you paid nothing out. Say what it was for in **Remarks**, and keep the bills with the cash.
6. Click one of:
   - **Close Day**: you are done for the day.
   - **Close & Hand Over**: another cashier takes this counter now. This closes your day and logs you out. See [Handing over a counter](#shift-handover).
7. Click **Print Day Close Slip** and keep the slip with the cash. It shows your count, the card and bank totals, and the expenditure.

## When the count does not match

A difference is not an error. The day goes to **Closing** (*Close sent for approval*), and a supervisor reviews your remarks and approves it. Then the day is **Closed**.

While you wait:

- **The counter is free.** The next cashier can open on it straight away.
- **You** cannot open a new day until your close is approved.

## About expenditure

- It is **one amount for the whole day**, entered when you close. It is not entered during the day.
- It lowers the cash the till should hold. **It is not posted to the accounts.** Give the bills to your supervisor or accountant so they can record the expenses.
- Once the day is closed, you cannot change it. A supervisor or System Manager corrects it by cancelling and amending the day.

## After closing

- The badge shows *Day not opened* (or *Close awaiting approval*).
- You cannot bill again until you open a new day.
- A closed day cannot be edited. If the count was wrong, a System Manager cancels and amends it. See [Problems and messages](#counter-troubleshooting).
