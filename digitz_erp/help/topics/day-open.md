# Opening your day

▶ **[Watch the three-minute video](#counter-video)** of a day at the counter.

Open your day at the start of your shift, before you bill anyone. Until you do, the system will not let you save invoices, returns, receipts or expenses.

## Before you start

- You must be on a **registered counter PC**. The badge shows the counter's name, such as *Counter 1 · DEV-0001*. If it says **Unregistered device**, ask a supervisor to register the PC.
- No one else may have a day open on this counter. If someone does, they close it first, or hand it over to you. See [Handing over a counter](#shift-handover).

## Steps

1. Click the badge at the top of the page, or open **Day Open** from the Medical Center workspace.
2. Check **Previous Close on this Counter** on the left. It shows who closed the last day here, when, and the **cash left in the till**.
3. Count the cash now in the till, in the **Count the Opening Cash** grid. Type how many of each note and coin you have. The **Opening Float** fills in from the count. You can also type the total straight into Opening Float.
4. Read the note under the float:
   - **Same as the cash left at the last close**: nothing changed overnight.
   - **Less / more than the last close**: cash was banked, taken out or added since. Explain it in **Remarks**.
5. Click **Open Day** and confirm.

The badge turns green (*Day open since …*) and you can bill.

## Taking over from the last cashier

If the last close on this counter was a handover, click **Take over this count**. It copies the outgoing cashier's note-by-note count into your grid and adds a remark. **Check it against the notes in the till before you open.** You are responsible for the till from the moment you open.

If the last close is still **waiting for a supervisor to approve its difference**, you can still open. The count shown is what was actually left in the till.

## Messages you may see

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| *This PC is not a registered counter* | Ask a supervisor to register this PC. |
| *Counter 1 is in use* | Another cashier's day is open here. They close it with **Close & Hand Over**, or a supervisor closes it for them. |
| *Your last close is waiting for approval* | A supervisor must approve your last close first. |
| *Your day is already open* | You are already open. Go to Day Close when your shift ends. |
