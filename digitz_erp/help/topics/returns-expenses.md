# Refunds and expenses

Money that goes **out** of the till is recorded as a **Sales Return** (a refund to a customer) or an **Expense Entry** (cash paid out). Both are on the Medical Center workspace under Daily Operations. Cashiers cannot make them: a supervisor or System Manager does. Open a section for the details.

## Refund a customer: Sales Return

A Sales Return reverses some or all of a submitted invoice: its revenue, its VAT and its stock.

1. Open **Sales Return**, then **+ Add Sales Return**, and pick the **Customer**.
2. Click **Get Items From Sales** and pick the invoice. Its lines come in with their charges, and only what has not been returned already.
3. Remove the lines that are not being returned, or lower their **Qty**.
4. Choose the **Payment Mode** the money goes back by, then **Save** and **Submit**.

- The charges and VAT come from the original invoice, so the refund matches what was charged. VAT is refunded on everything but GOV, as it was charged.
- The returned quantity is recorded against the invoice, so it cannot be returned twice.

## Cash paid out: Expense Entry

An Expense Entry records an expense and how it was paid.

Add the expense lines, choose the **Payment Mode** and **Save** and **Submit**. Unlike the Day Close **Expenditure** figure, an Expense Entry is posted to the accounts.

Use one or the other for the same payment, not both: either enter it as an Expense Entry during the day, or as **Expenditure** when closing the day (see [Closing your day](#day-close)).

## How they affect the till

A cash refund or cash expense lowers the expected cash of the day it was saved in.

At Day Close they appear as **− Cash Refunds** and **− Cash Paid Out**. Only **submitted** documents whose payment mode's **Mode** is **Cash** count, and only in the **open day** of the counter they were saved on.

So enter them **on the counter's PC, while that day is open**. One saved on another PC, or with no day open, belongs to no till: no Day Close accounts for it, and the till looks short.

In the reports, a Sales Return comes off the sales on the date of the return. See [Medical Center reports](#medical-center-reports).
