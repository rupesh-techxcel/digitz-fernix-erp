# Supervisor tasks

## Who is a supervisor

Anyone with one of these can supervise:

- the **System Manager** or **Management** role, or
- a **Cashier** record with **Is Supervisor** ticked.

Supervisors can register PCs, approve cash differences and close other cashiers' days. The counter badge shows them the number of closes waiting for approval.

## Approve a cash difference

When a cashier's count does not match the expected cash, their day waits in **Closing**.

1. Open **Day Close**. Supervisors see **Pending Approvals** at the bottom. You can also click the orange pill on the Medical Center Dashboard.
2. Check the **Expected**, **Counted** and **Difference** columns and read the cashier's **Remarks**.
3. Click **Approve & Close** and confirm.

You can also open the Counter Session itself and click **Approve & Close** there. Your name is recorded as **Difference Approved By** and printed on the Day Close Slip.

> When you close **your own** day with a difference as a supervisor, it still waits for a *different* supervisor to approve it.

## Close a day for a cashier who has left

If a cashier leaves without closing, their counter stays blocked. No one else can open a day on it.

1. Open **Day Close**. **Open Days** lists every other cashier's open day with the cash expected **right now**.
2. Click **Close for Cashier** on that row.
3. Count the till in the dialog. **Counted Cash** fills in from the count.
4. Enter a **Reason**. This is required and is printed on the slip.
5. Click **Count & Close**.

The day closes at once. **Any difference is approved in your name**, and you are recorded as **Closed By**. The counter is free for the next cashier.

## Register a PC as a counter

Do this once for each PC that takes money.

1. Sign in on the PC itself.
2. Click the red **Unregistered device** badge. Choose the **Counter** and click **Register**.

![The Register this device dialog](/assets/digitz_erp/images/help/register-device.png)

The PC gets its own number (DEV-0007). Everything billed on it is recorded against that counter from now on. See [Setting up counters](#counter-setup) for details, including how to retire a PC.

## Good practice

- **Approve differences the same day,** while the cashier can still explain them.
- **Bill cash only on an open day.** Supervisors are not *forced* to open a day. But cash you take without an open day of your own does not belong to any till, and no Day Close will account for it.
- **Check the Counters panel** on the Medical Center Dashboard during the day. It shows which counters are open, who is on each and the cash in each till.
